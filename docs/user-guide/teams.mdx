## Workspace roles

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access. Can delete the workspace. Only one per workspace. |
| **Admin** | Create, edit, archive, and delete any resource. Manage members. |
| **Member** | Create and view. Cannot archive, delete, or manage members. |

---

## Inviting a team member

1. Go to **Settings → Team** (or **Settings → Members**).
2. Click **Invite member**.
3. Enter their email address and select a role (Admin or Member).
4. Click **Send invitation**.

The invitee receives an email with a link. When they click it, they are prompted to create an account (if they don't have one) and then added to your workspace.

---

## Invitation lifecycle

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Pending` | Email sent, not yet accepted |
| `Accepted` | Member has joined the workspace |
| `Expired` | Invitation link is older than 7 days (resend from the same page) |

---

## Changing a member's role

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Find the member and click the role dropdown.
3. Select the new role — the change takes effect immediately.

<Note>
You cannot change the **Owner** role. To transfer ownership, contact support.
</Note>

---

## Removing a member

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Click the ⋯ menu next to the member.
3. Select **Remove from workspace**.

Removing a member does not delete any content they created — projects, transactions, and actors they added remain in your workspace.

---

## Solo mode vs. team mode

Project Manager starts in **solo mode** — one user, full access. Team mode activates automatically when the first invitation is accepted. In team mode:

- Role-based access is enforced on all API routes
- The **Members** section appears in Settings
- The audit log records who made each change

There is no manual toggle — the switch is seamless.
