## Welcome to Project Manager

Project Manager is your **personal operational OS** — a single workspace where you run projects, track money, and manage people without switching tools.

This guide walks you through each module from an end-user perspective. If you're looking for technical setup, see [Installation](/getting-started/installation).

---

## Workspace model

When you sign up, a **workspace** is created automatically. Everything you create — projects, actors, transactions — lives inside your workspace.

When you invite team members, they join your workspace with a specific role:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access, can delete the workspace |
| **Admin** | Create/edit/archive anything, manage members |
| **Member** | Read and create; cannot delete or archive |

## Onboarding checklist

When you first log in, a checklist guides you through the key setup steps:

1. **Create your first project** — gives you a project to attach everything else to
2. **Add an actor** — add at least one client, collaborator, or vendor
3. **Log your first transaction** — record income or an expense
4. **Invite a team member** — optional; activates team mode
5. **Set up workspace settings** — set your name, currency defaults, invoice branding

The checklist disappears once all five steps are complete.
