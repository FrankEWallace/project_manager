## Overview

The **Analytics** module gives you two views:
1. **Portfolio dashboard** — rolled-up KPIs across all your projects
2. **Per-project analytics** — detailed financials and progress for a single project

Both update in real time as you log transactions and complete milestones.

---

## Portfolio dashboard

The dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. It shows:

### KPI row

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total revenue** | Sum of all income transactions in your workspace |
| **Total expenses** | Sum of all expense transactions |
| **Net profit** | Revenue minus expenses |
| **Active projects** | Count of projects with `Active` status |

Each KPI shows a **trend arrow** comparing the current month to the previous month:
- ↑ Green — improvement
- ↓ Red — regression
- — Grey — no prior data or no change

### Status summary

A quick-glance row showing counts for: Active, Completed, On Hold, Overdue. The Overdue count turns red when > 0.

### Milestone completion rate

Shows what percentage of all milestones across your workspace have been completed.

### Recent projects table

The 6 most recently created projects with status, health, priority, and due date. Click any row to open the project.

### Due in 14 days

Projects with a due date within the next two weeks. Overdue projects appear at the top in red.

### Upcoming payments

Transactions with a future date flagged as pending — so you know what cash is coming in and going out.

---

## Per-project analytics

Open any project and go to the **Analytics** tab to see:

- Income vs. expenses over time (monthly bar chart)
- Category breakdown for expenses
- Budget vs. actual bar
- Milestone completion timeline

---

## Reading trends

Trend percentages are calculated as:

```
pct = ((current_month - previous_month) / previous_month) * 100
```

If there is no previous month data, the trend shows "No prior data" instead of a percentage.

<Note>
Trends compare **calendar months** — current month-to-date vs. the full previous month. Early in the month, numbers will look lower than last month until the month fills out.
</Note>
