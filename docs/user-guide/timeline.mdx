## Overview

The **Timeline** module gives you a visual overview of your work across time. It is read-only — you edit projects and milestones in the Projects module; the timeline reflects those changes automatically.

---

## Views

### Roadmap view

A horizontal Gantt-style view showing each project as a bar spanning its start date to its due date. Milestones appear as diamonds on the bar at their due date.

Colour coding:
- **Blue** — Active
- **Green** — Completed
- **Yellow** — On Hold
- **Red** — Overdue (active and past due date)
- **Grey** — Draft or Cancelled

### Calendar view

A monthly calendar where each project due date and milestone appears as an event. Click any event to jump to that project or milestone.

---

## Navigating the timeline

- Use the **← →** arrows to move between months (calendar) or scroll left/right (roadmap).
- Use the **Today** button to jump back to the current date.
- Projects without a start date appear anchored to their creation date in roadmap view.
- Projects without a due date do not appear on the timeline.

---

## Tips

<Tip>
Set due dates on your projects and milestones to get the most out of the timeline. Projects without due dates are invisible here.
</Tip>

<Tip>
Use the roadmap view for quarterly planning and the calendar view for tracking week-to-week deliverables.
</Tip>
